# Demo video script: The Incense Ball

Target length: about 2:45. The hard limit is 3:00. Record the Lens Studio preview and narrate over it.
Judges won't run the project, so the video has to show every step working, plus the problem, who it's for, and why it needs glasses.

## Before recording

1. Open the project in Lens Studio 5.23.2. In the Preview panel, use the SPECS device and Interactive Preview. Make the Preview panel as large as you can.
2. Click the preview's refresh button so the Lens starts fresh and the title is showing. Don't touch anything until you start recording.
3. Right-drag to look slightly down until the plinth, the ball, the title, and the **Take apart** button are all in frame. This is the visitor's starting view.
4. Press **Cmd+Shift+5**, choose **Record Selected Portion**, frame the Preview panel, and under **Options** pick your microphone.
5. Do one silent practice run first. The rolling drag is the shot that matters most.

## Shots

| Time | On screen (what you do) | Narration (say this) |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:12 | The start view: ball on the plinth, title, Take apart button. Don't touch anything yet. | "This silver incense ball is more than 1,200 years old. You can roll it any way you like and the ember inside never spills. Here's why." |
| 0:12–0:32 | Still the start view. After 5 seconds the "Pinch and drag to roll" hint appears on its own. | "In a museum it's palm-sized and sits behind glass, so you never see the trick. The Incense Ball is a one-object AR exhibit for SPECS, for museum visitors and students who wonder how ancient machines work. A 30-centimeter copy stands on a plinth in your room." |
| 0:32–1:05 | Click-drag the ball left and right until the shell is upside down, then let go. Keep still for 2–3 seconds. | "Pinch and drag to roll. The shell turns see-through, the two rings swing on their pivots, and the cup stays level. The two rings turn so the cup doesn't have to. I'll let go upside down: the cup is still level and the smoke still rises straight up. Then the shell fades back to silver." |
| 1:05–1:30 | Click **Take apart**. When the parts settle, press A for a step left, look at the parts, then press D to step back. | "Take apart opens the shell like a clam and slides the rings and cup out along their pivots: five parts at half size, in two labeled tiers, with the pivot pins lit so you can see the two axes at right angles. The exhibit stays put in the room while I move." |
| 1:30–1:55 | Click **Outer ring**, wait for the card, then click it again. Then click **Incense cup**, and click it again. | "Pinch a part to bring it forward at full size. A one-line card says what it does, and the other parts dim. Pinch again and it goes back." |
| 1:55–2:10 | Click **Put together**, then roll the ball once more. | "Put together returns every part exactly where it was, and the ball rolls again." |
| 2:10–2:38 | Show `devpost/design-brief.md` for a moment, then the LEAF results table in the README or `Assets/Tests/RESULTS.md`. | "I planned it with the plan-lens interview: 90 centimeters away and a little below eye level, big pinch targets, and light colors, because black is invisible on the glasses. I also wrote five done criteria I could check in the preview. CLAD built it through the Lens Studio MCP, and LEAF tests drive real pinches and drags. All five pass, including the cup staying level on every frame. Comfort, reach, and readability still need real glasses." |
| 2:38–2:45 | Back to the assembled ball. | "The Incense Ball: an ancient machine you can roll in your own room." |
